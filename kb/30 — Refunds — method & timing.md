# Refunds: method & timing

Refunds go back to your **original payment method**. We process them within **14 days of receiving your return** — often sooner. Your bank or Klarna may take a few extra days to show it.

- **Outbound shipping:** refunded for full-order returns and for valid 14-day withdrawals; not refunded on partial change-of-mind returns.
- **Return postage:** not refunded for change-of-mind (see "Return shipping: who pays").
- **Store credit:** available instead of a cash refund on request, issued as soon as the return is processed.
- **Sale items** may be refunded as store credit only — see "Sale & clearance items".
