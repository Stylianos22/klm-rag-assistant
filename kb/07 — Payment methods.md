# Payment methods

Northbound accepts the following payment methods, all in euros (EUR):

- **Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express.
- **Wallets:** PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
- **Klarna (pay later):** Pay in 30 days, or Pay in 3 interest-free instalments. Subject to Klarna's approval; see Klarna's terms at checkout.
- **Northbound gift cards.**

All card payments are protected by 3-D Secure verification. We never store full card numbers. If a payment is declined, see "Declined or failed payments".
