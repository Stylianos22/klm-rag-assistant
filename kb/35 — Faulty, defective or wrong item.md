# Faulty, defective, or wrong item

If something arrives **faulty, damaged in transit, or it isn't what you ordered**, we'll put it right:

- Report it within **30 days** for a **free replacement or a full refund**.
- **Northbound pays** the return shipping — we send a free label.
- Beyond 30 days, items are covered for up to **2 years** against manufacturing defects (not damage from normal wear or misuse), in line with EU law.

Please include your **order number and a photo** of the issue to speed things up. This is separate from changing your mind — those returns are covered in "Returns policy & return window".
