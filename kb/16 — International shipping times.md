# International shipping times

Orders are dispatched within **1 business day**. Estimated delivery, counted from dispatch:

- **Netherlands:** 1–2 business days
- **Belgium, Germany, Luxembourg:** 2–3 business days
- **Rest of the EU:** 3–7 business days
- **Non-EU Europe:** 5–10 business days

A tracking link is emailed when your order ships. Estimates may be longer during sale periods and public holidays.
