# Sale & clearance items: return exceptions

Discounted items follow different return rules from full-price items:

- **Regular sale / discounted items:** returnable within **14 days** of delivery (shorter than the standard 30 days), and refunded as **store credit only** — not to your original payment method.
- **Clearance / items marked "Final sale":** **not returnable.**

Your statutory rights for **faulty** goods are unaffected by this — a faulty sale item is still covered (see "Faulty, defective or wrong item").
