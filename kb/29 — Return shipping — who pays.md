# Return shipping: who pays

**Changed your mind / standard return:** you pay return postage. Use our prepaid label (€4.95, deducted from your refund) or your own tracked courier.

**Faulty, damaged, or wrong item:** Northbound pays. We send a free return label — you are never charged to return something that was our fault.

**EU right of withdrawal:** you cover the direct cost of returning the goods, unless we told you otherwise.

Always start the return from Your account so the right label and address are issued.
