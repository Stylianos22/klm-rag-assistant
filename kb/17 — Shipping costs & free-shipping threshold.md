# Shipping costs & free shipping

Standard shipping rates:

- **Netherlands:** €4.95 — **free on orders over €50**.
- **Rest of the EU:** €6.95 — **free on orders over €75**.
- **Express** (where available): added at checkout.

Within the EU there are no extra customs charges. Orders outside the EU may incur import duties or taxes, paid by the customer.

Example: a €45 order shipped to Germany is below the €75 EU free-shipping threshold, so standard EU shipping of €6.95 applies.
