# Your EU right of withdrawal (14-day cooling-off)

Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive you have a **14-day right of withdrawal**: you may cancel your order for **any reason — no explanation needed** — within 14 days of receiving the goods.

How it works:
- Tell us you are withdrawing within 14 days of delivery.
- Return the goods within 14 days of telling us.
- We refund you within 14 days of receiving the goods back, including the standard outbound shipping cost.

This statutory right always applies. Northbound's 30-day returns policy is offered on top of it. Exceptions allowed by law: personalised goods and hygiene-sealed items once unsealed.
